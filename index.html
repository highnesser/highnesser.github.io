A web-accessibility product with zero customers and no acquisition motion. I owned the whole path to revenue. How I operate
// the way a founding growth hire has to think

I own the whole funnel, not one channel

Positioning, landing pages, email, ads, webinars, launches. On ADACertify I used whichever channel the funnel needed to get from zero to paying customers. The constraint moves; I follow it wherever it goes.

I find where it actually breaks

The apparent problem is rarely the real one. ADACertify looked like an acquisition miss; the real issue was measurement hiding the true funnel. I diagnose against the backend and billing data, not the surface dashboard.

I write and ship, I don't hand off

Ads, pages, email sequences, launch copy, all mine. No waiting on a design or content team to test an idea. That's what makes a 0-to-1 motion move fast enough to matter.

Validate before scaling

Prove demand with a small controlled test, then pour budget into what's real. Every offer and channel gets an honest verdict: scale it, reangle it, or cut it. No sunk-cost romance.

The Measurement Catch
Why the dashboard isn’t the source of truth
DIFFERENTIATOR

My edge isn’t just running campaigns, it’s catching when the numbers lie.

On ADACertify, the ad platform reported 28 leads and zero sales, it looked like the ads were failing. But the real backend showed 69 registrations and 11 sales of a $397 product. The pixel was misconfigured. On the Google side, I caught page-view and video-engagement actions mis-set as primary conversions, inflating the numbers. I reconcile against the backend, not the dashboard, which is where most growth decisions quietly go wrong.
